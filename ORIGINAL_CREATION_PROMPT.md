# Live Vibe - Original Creation Prompt

## Project Vision
Create "Live Vibe" - The ultimate AI-powered artist ecosystem, empowering independent artists, event organizers, and fans through the power of AI, automation, and community.

## Core Benefits
Live Vibe empowers independent artists to:
1. **Effortlessly showcase their talent** - Professional portfolios and AI-generated content
2. **Connect with event organizers** - Smart matching and booking system
3. **Grow their fanbase** - Social media integration and discovery tools
4. **Earn consistently** - Secure payment processing and booking management

All achieved through AI-driven content creation, smart bookings, and automated marketing—all in one powerful platform.

## How It Works - User Journey

### Step 1: Set Up Your Artist Profile
Tell the world who you are—add your bio, performance samples, availability, and pricing so organizers and fans can discover you.

### Step 2: Upload Your Work and Let AI Create
Share your music, lyrics, or artwork, and our AI will instantly turn it into stunning videos and visuals—ready to post anywhere.

### Step 3: Get Discovered and Booked
Sit back while event organizers find and book you through smart recommendations that match your vibe, genre, and schedule.

### Step 4: Promote Yourself Effortlessly
Use AI-generated social content and insights to grow your fanbase, boost your visibility, and keep the spotlight on you.

### Step 5: Earn, Connect, and Collaborate
Get paid securely, join creative communities, and team up with other artists or go live with your fans—right from the platform.

## Key Features Required

### For Artists
- **Artist Profile Creation** - Comprehensive profiles with portfolio, social links, genres, instruments
- **AI Content Generation** - Turn music/lyrics into stunning videos and visuals
- **Portfolio Management** - Upload and showcase work (images, audio, video, documents)
- **Booking Management** - Receive and manage event booking requests
- **Payment Processing** - Secure payment handling with escrow protection
- **Social Media Integration** - Connect Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Spotify, etc.
- **Subscription Tiers** - Freemium, Pro, and Elite plans with different features

### For Event Organizers/Promoters
- **Event Creation** - Create and manage events with detailed requirements
- **Artist Discovery** - Search and filter artists by location, genre, budget
- **Booking System** - Send booking requests and manage contracts
- **Payment Processing** - Secure payment with platform commission
- **Event Management** - Calendar integration and logistics tools

### Technical Requirements
- **Authentication System** - Email/password and OAuth (Spotify integration)
- **Database** - Supabase with proper RLS policies
- **File Storage** - Profile photos and portfolio uploads
- **Payment Processing** - Square integration for secure transactions
- **Responsive Design** - Mobile-first approach with Tailwind CSS
- **Modern UI** - shadcn/ui components with premium aesthetics

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Independent artists (musicians, visual artists, performers)
- **Secondary**: Event organizers, venue managers, promoters, curators
- **Tertiary**: Fans and music enthusiasts

## Monetization Strategy
- **Commission-based**: 10% standard commission on bookings
- **Subscription Plans**: 
  - Artists: Free, Pro ($15/month), Elite ($35/month)
  - Organizers: Free, Pro ($25/month), Elite ($60/month)
- **AI Generations**: Limited free generations, unlimited on paid plans

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- **Backend**: Supabase (Database + Auth + Storage)
- **Payments**: Square API
- **Deployment**: Netlify/Vercel

## Success Metrics
- Average artist earnings: $2,500/month
- Time to first booking: 24 hours
- Artist portfolio completion rate: 90%+
- Platform commission: 10% standard, reduced for premium tiers
